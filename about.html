About the Club

A community of competitive programmers practicing algorithms, competing in weekly mock rounds, and preparing for ICPC regionals.

What We Do

We hold weekly mock contests, upsolving discussions, and topic workshops covering core algorithmic families.

  • Saturday Mocks: 5 problems, 2 hours, ICPC-style scoring.
  • Upsolve Sessions: Every unsolved contest problem gets dissected.
  • Topic Workshops: Deep dives into DP, graphs, and advanced data structures.

ICPC Preparation

Every autumn we form our regional teams. Practice routines include:

  • Timed practice on the Kattis and Codeforces Gym archives of past regionals.
  • Team strategy: problem triage, parallel work, and penalty-time minimization.
  • Stress-testing and debugging drills under strict timed conditions.

Ranking Tiers

Club ratings mirror the official Codeforces rating tiers:

Newbie

Rating < 1200

Focus on implementation speed, basic math, sorting, and simulation problems.

Pupil

Rating 1200 – 1399

Learn greedy exchange arguments, prefix sums, two pointers, and binary search on answer.

Specialist

Rating 1400 – 1599

Master BFS/DFS traversals, Dijkstra, DSU, and classic 1D/2D dynamic programming.

Expert

Rating 1600 – 1899

Advanced segment trees, Fenwick trees, fast modular arithmetic, and tree DP.

Contest Rules

  • Teams: 1–3 members per team during official mock contests.
  • Scoring: Most problems solved wins; ties are broken by penalty time.
  • Penalty: +20 minutes per incorrect verdict on an eventual accepted problem.
  • Integrity: No generative AI, no internet search during live rounds — written reference templates are permitted.
